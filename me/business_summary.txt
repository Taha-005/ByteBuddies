Byte Buddies - Business Summary

Byte Buddies is a weekend coding and robotics school for kids aged 8 to 14, based in Hamra, Beirut. Our mission is to help kids become confident creators with technology, not just users of it. Every class is hands-on and project-based: kids write real code, build real robots, and leave each term with something they made themselves.

We run three programs, grouped by age. Byte Starters (ages 8-10) introduces coding with Scratch and simple robotics with LEGO SPIKE kits. Byte Builders (ages 11-12) moves on to Python basics and electronics with micro:bit boards and sensors. Byte Makers (ages 13-14) works on bigger Python projects, simple game development, and competition-style robotics challenges. No prior experience is needed for any program, and every new student can book one free trial class before enrolling.

Byte Buddies was founded in 2023 by Lina Haddad, a computer engineer who spent six years as a software developer, and Karim Mansour, a physics teacher and FIRST LEGO League coach. Our instructors are university engineering and computer science students trained by Lina and Karim, and every class has at most 8 kids per instructor.

What makes us different: small groups, all equipment provided (laptops, robot kits, and electronics stay at the studio), a written progress note for parents at mid-term and end of term, and a Demo Day at the end of every term where kids present their projects to families.

Practical details: Classes run on Saturdays and Sundays only. Each class is 2 hours, in one of three time slots: 9:30-11:30, 12:30-14:30, or 15:00-17:00. Our studio is on the 2nd floor of Cedar House, Bliss Street, Hamra, Beirut. The front desk is open Fridays 14:00-18:00 and weekends 9:00-17:00. Contact: hello@bytebuddies.lb.

Pricing works per term. A term is 10 weekly 2-hour classes. Byte Starters costs $180 per term, Byte Builders $200 per term, and Byte Makers $220 per term, with all equipment included. There is a one-time registration fee of $20 for new students. Payment is made in full at registration, by cash or bank transfer.
